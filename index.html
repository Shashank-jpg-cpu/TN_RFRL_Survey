Centimeter / Sub-Meter Precision Guide

Standard phone GPS autonomous chips achieve 2–4m accuracy in open sky. Here is how professional forest boundary surveyors achieve Centimeter (1–2 cm) & Sub-Meter Precision:

Method 1: 20-Epoch Multi-Sample Averaging (Built-In)

Stand steady over the boundary cairn stone for 15 seconds and tap "20-Epoch Precision Average". The app averages 20 satellite readings, excludes multipath noise, and cuts GPS jitter down to < 0.8 meters automatically without any extra hardware.

Method 2: Bluetooth RTK / DGPS Rover (True 1–2 cm)

For true legal cadastral boundary precision (1 to 2 cm), connect an external Bluetooth RTK receiver (e.g. Trimble Catalyst, Emlid Reach RX, Garmin GLO 2, CHCNAV):

  1. Turn on Android Developer Options → "Select mock location app" (use free Lefebure NTRIP Client or Emlid Flow).
  2. Connect to the Survey of India CORS Network via mobile data.
  3. The RTK receiver locks into RTK FIX (1cm accuracy) and feeds directly into Android's system location.
  4. This web app will instantly detect the RTK signal and record 1-centimeter cairn coordinates!
Method 3: Visual High-Res Satellite Snapping (15–30 cm)

The app loads Google Satellite Hybrid (22x zoom). Physical white cairn stones are clearly visible in the satellite photo. You can drag and micro-adjust any captured point directly onto the visible cairn stone.

Install TN RFRL Survey App

Install this app directly onto your Android phone:

1 Tap the 3 dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome on your phone.
2 Tap "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").

The TN RFRL Survey icon will appear on your phone's home screen.

It runs full-screen, caches all maps offline in forest areas, and connects to high-accuracy GPS!
